Common Questions

Everything you need to know before your appointment.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book directly through our website using the "Book Now" button or by calling our service line. We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance to secure your preferred time slot.

What are the on-site requirements for mobile detailing?

We require a flat workspace with enough room to open all vehicle doors comfortably. Access to a water spigot and a standard electrical outlet is preferred, though we can provide our own power/water for an additional setup fee if notified in advance.

How long does a typical detail take?

Estimated times vary by package: Interior details usually take 2-4 hours, while Full Premium details can take 5-8 hours depending on the vehicle's condition and size.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. Payment is processed upon completion of the service and your final inspection.

What results should I realistically expect?

We aim for a "like-new" restoration. However, deep-set stains, permanent paint damage, or severe upholstery wear may not be fully reversible. We provide a pre-service assessment to manage expectations for every vehicle.

Service Guarantees & Limitations

Our Satisfaction Promise

We guarantee professional care for your vehicle. If you are not satisfied with any part of our service, we will address it on the spot or schedule a complimentary touch-up within 48 hours.

Realistic Limitations

While we use premium products, we cannot guarantee 100% removal of deep biological stains, permanent paint etching (from bird droppings/acid rain), or existing mechanical damage. Pre-existing structural issues or loose trim are not our responsibility.

Booking & Cancellation Policy

Clear terms for a smooth scheduling experience.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

We require at least 24 hours' notice for any cancellation or rescheduling. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a 25% service fee. Rescheduling within the 24-hour window is permitted once without penalty, subject to availability.

Deposit & Refund Rules

Premium packages may require a non-refundable $50 deposit to secure long-duration slots. Deposits are applied toward your final balance. Refunds for completed services are not provided; however, we will perform rework as per our satisfaction guarantee.

Weather & Site Access Contingencies

In the event of severe weather (heavy rain, snow, extreme heat), we reserve the right to reschedule for the safety of our technicians and your vehicle. If we arrive and cannot access the vehicle or site, a "No-Show" fee may apply.

02

04
